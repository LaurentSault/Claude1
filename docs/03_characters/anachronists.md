## 1. Reading Rules

- **The canon wins.** Every ID, date, age, rung, boss figure and fate below comes from CANON §3c, §4b–c, §8, §9 and §13. Where this file elaborates, it elaborates inside those facts. World_rules_and_lore owns the laws, the group chronology (lore §8) and the texts of in-world documents (lore §13). This file owns the people.
- **One template per member.** Each dossier gives, in order: identity card · before (the origin-era life) · the rift · arrival and rise · what they stole or introduced · their stake in the portal secret · red line · psychology and wound · their strongest honest argument, and the game's answer · tells and clues by chapter · key scenes · a beat excerpt · boss tie-in and battle lines · fate in both branches.
- **Staging rules for the cabal (binding for the scenario docs):**
  1. **Future words in private.** Among themselves the members speak a French salted with their own centuries ("kilometre", "photograph", "the twentieth century"). The Table at the Val is the only room in Paris where they can do that (lore §8). In front of natives CANON §16g binds them like everyone else, and a member who slips in public is tagged like Min-jun.
  2. **2049 stays in its four scenes** (LAW-14: CH-07 hint, CH-12, the CH-E1 duel, the CH-E2 dossier), with at most 6 boxes of exposition in each. Brücke's backstory in §8 is writers' reference, not script.
  3. **Archon before the Revelation.** In cabal interludes before CH-12, Archon is **the Premier**. He is seen from behind in a high-backed chair and speaks as `[N:The Premier]` in the no-portrait box. In public he is the Baron d'Orsenne, with his portrait and the name tab "Baron d'Orsenne" (CANON §16h). At the Revelation the chair turns.
  4. **Expressions** use the base set plus Archon's canon `cold`. The style guide's proposed cabal extras (§3.3 there) fall back to the base set until adopted.
  5. **"Kill" is said, never shown** (CANON §16e). Human enemies are routed, subdued or arrested.
  6. **Every script excerpt is a beat excerpt** in dialogue_style_guide §2.2 format. The scenario docs own the final scene IDs, headers and STATE footers. Battle lines follow the bark strip rule (≤ 30 characters, style guide §5.1).

## 2. La Confrérie de l'Heure Immobile

### 2.1 Name, sigil and rings

**Name.** *La Confrérie de l'Heure Immobile*, the **Brotherhood of the Stilled Hour**, named by Archon when the Pact was sworn (21 Jun 1826). "Anachronists" is Min-jun's word (CH-06). Archon sneers at it in the cabal interludes ("a schoolboy's word for a priesthood") and uses it himself in CH-12 (CANON §8).

**Sigil.** A clock face whose two hands are fused into one upright key at XII, ringed by seven dots (CANON §8). Archon copied it in 2012 from the bow of the Ornate Brass Key (KEY-01). The friends cast that bow after the sigil in 1834, so the sign has no first author (knot 2, LAW-16). Each member reads it differently. For Archon it is *the hour stopped by a key*: the seal. For Delorme the seven dots are *her* Clefs, and the key in the middle is his. Vane thinks it is "a rather good logo". Julien cannot look at it.

**Binding rings** (LAW-11). Each ring is a bead of Clef stone set in black enamel and engraved with the sigil. Delorme forged them all. The colour of the thread round each bezel is CANON §5g's Hour colour.

| Member | Thread | How it is worn | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Archon | None: plain black | Right hand, fourth finger; never removed | 21 Jun 1820 |
| Delorme | Ochre-gold | Left hand, under the fingerless glove she paints in | 21 Jun 1820 |
| Marthe | White | On a cord with her rosary, since a Daughter of Charity wears no ring; it hangs below the crucifix, which is how Min-jun first sees the sigil on a nun (CH-07) | 21 Jun 1826 |

| Brücke | Gunmetal | Under a watchmaker's leather finger-stall at the bench | Jan 1832 |

**How a sanction is given.** An Hour proposes at the Table. The Premier answers "sanctioned", or says nothing; silence is refusal. The Hours' votes are counsel only. Archon has overruled the Table outright twice: in August 1830, when Delorme voted against hiring the Grey Hand and Marthe spoke against it, and on 13 Nov 1833, when Vane voted against seizing Théo and Marthe spoke against it. Each Hour reports to the Premier alone, so no member knows everything the others do (lore §8). That is how Brücke can rewrite a specification behind Julien's back. **Unsanctioned acts** are rung 5 (Vane), rung 8 (Brücke), Brücke's lethal tuning in rung 10, and every Hit (BOSS-41; secrecy_and_trust §2.8.3). After each one, Archon's rebuke is the next tier box.

### 2.3 The Pact of the Hour

Sworn at the Val on 21 Jun 1826 by Archon, Delorme and Marthe, on the anniversary of Archon's arrival (CANON §3c, §8). Vane swore it in Jan 1828. Julien swore it in Aug 1830 with his ring in his fist, and Brücke in Jan 1832 with his eyes on the clock. Every **21 June** the members dine at the Château d'Orsenne and repeat the rules aloud: the **Solstice Dinner**.

| Rule | Who keeps it | Who breaks it, and when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Never go home | All. Every member except Brücke could have gone home through Archon's Heart, and each refused (LAW-05) | Brücke cannot keep or break it: his far end will never exist |
| 2. Never reveal **that you come from the future** to a native | Vane, Julien, Delorme and Marthe, to the end | **Archon** breaks it at the Revelation (CH-12), in front of the whole party. Marthe's CH-11 note (lore §13.17) comes as close as she dares: "I have sworn an oath I have not finished breaking" |
| 3. Never harm another traveller | Vane, Julien, Marthe | **Brücke**, privately, from CH-06 ("He is not a traveller. He is a source."). **Archon** openly, from the abduction (CH-09): "he is not one of us until he chooses to stay" (CANON §8) |

**The hole in the Pact.** It protects travellers from one another and says nothing about natives. Lucile, Théo, Corbel and every Grey Hand recruit stand outside it. Marthe has said so at the Table since 1826, and nobody has ever answered her. The friends' **Pact of the Door** (LAW-22) is its mirror: one pact hides a door, the other builds one, and the friends' pact is sworn for a man none of them can keep.

### 2.8 How they recruit

## 3. The Escalation Ladder (Addendum A-1)

CANON §4c is binding for every rung's number, chapter, tier, executor, motive (**M1** exposure in 1833 · **M2** the portal: he must never go home · **SIG** the signature or the seal) and sanction. This section adds who gave each order, the method, how Min-jun survives, and what each rung costs the cabal.

**The shape of the climb.** The brief's sequence is discredit → sabotage → framing → kidnapping → assassination → sealing. In the game it runs: **discredit** (rungs 3, 7) → **sabotage** (4, 6, 8) → **framing** (5) → **kidnapping** (9, 11) → **assassination** (10, 13, 14, 16) → **sealing** (15). Three containment rungs frame the climb: the Search (1), the Leash (2) and the Offer (12). The framing comes chronologically early because **Vane jumped the ladder without sanction** (R-08). Vane never knew that Archon needed Min-jun in Paris, so to him a foreign agitator put on a ship was the merciful answer. That is why Archon's rebuke is so cold: an expulsion would have taken the string out of the city.
