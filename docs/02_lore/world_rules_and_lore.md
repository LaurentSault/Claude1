## 1. Purpose and Reading Rules

This file is the game's physics and its memory: what a character can know and when, why every Secrecy point and every Clef exists, and what a rift sounds and looks like.

1. **The supernatural is acoustic** (CANON §1, Tone Rule 5). Nobody casts spells. People strike stone, hold chords and record overtones.
2. **There is no narrator** (Tone Rule 6). Everything here reaches the player through a character (a lecture runs to 3 boxes at most, CANON §16a), a document (§13), or something shown. 2049 material stays inside LAW-14's four scenes.
3. **The words are fixed.** "Window" means the physical, passable opening; "resonant" means the latent state (LAW-04). A "Clef" is a Paris anchor; a "far end" is a fragment on the future side. "Anachronists" is Min-jun's word and no native uses it before CH-06.
4. **Three questions drive the mystery:** *How do I get home? Who built the Door? Why do they want me dead?* §12 schedules every answer.

## 2. Cosmology: A City Tuned Like an Instrument

Under the Left Bank lies a bed of unusually pure *liais*, the hard, fine-grained limestone that quarrymen prized for steps and sills. Seven lenses of it ring when struck and go on ringing far longer than stone should. These are the **Clefs** (§4). Every law follows from treating Paris as one instrument:

| Part of the instrument | In the world | Law |
|---|---|---|
| Strings | The seven Clefs, each with its own pitch | LAW-02 |
| Sympathetic strings | **Far ends**: fragments of Clef stone lying in the future, which sing when Paris sings | LAW-03 |
| The hand that plays | A **ring**: a Resonance Date (a mass upheaval) or a deliberate **strike** | LAW-04 |
| Pegs | Delorme's **tablature**: seven panels that pull each Clef onto its note | LAW-08 |
| A mechanical player | Brücke's **Engine**, able to hold a chord for hours | LAW-09 |
| A fingerprint | The **signature**: an overtone carried by a traveller whose far end is resonant | LAW-12 |
| The room's echo | **Echoes**: the city's recent noise printed onto stone | LAW-07 |
| The score being played | History: **bar-lines** resist change, **ossia** bends, **knots** hold | LAW-15–16 |

Rifts are **heard before they are seen**, so the person who notices them is a musician: Min-jun's Perfect Pitch is a trained ear in a world that hums. And **the network is alive.** It reawakens in 1814, when a traveller first listens to it, grows stronger with every experiment, and dies on 22 Dec 1833 (LAW-13). Echoes, Lecterns and rifts are symptoms of that life.

**Lecterns.** Where the network's hum pools at a node, a rift-touched ear hears a held note and the eye supplies a faintly glowing music stand (CANON §11h). "Rift-touched" means anyone who has stood within earshot of a living far end; for Min-jun, that is from the moment he hears the hum through the closet crack. The prologue's Lectern is the Door's own hum. Where Min-jun is absent, the Lectern still saves: the player hears the pooled note, while the characters feel only a warm corner ("like a cat at a ghost", overworld_and_towns §4.1). In CH-E2 the Lecterns are afterglow, amber, and one goes dark after each major beat. CH-E1 has none, because the only far end in Seoul is dead.

## 3. The Laws of Time, Expanded (LAW-01 – LAW-27)

Each law gives **Plain** (the rule in plain language), **Mechanism**, **Examples**, **Edge cases**, **In play / story** and **Chapters**. Where CANON §9 already states a fact, the entry points to it instead of repeating it; laws with their own section below are kept short.

### LAW-01 Rifts
- **Plain:** A rift is a doorway made of sound. A future moment and a Paris moment, tuned alike, sing each other open.
- **Mechanism:** Sympathetic resonance. When a Clef rings, every fragment of its stone rings too, and at the instant they share, the space between them speaks through. The seam takes the outline of the nearest built opening (an arch, the Door), or otherwise a tall rectangle, and hums at its far end's pitch.
- **Examples:** The hum through the closet crack (CH-P). Julien followed "a bass note nobody was playing" into a catacomb gallery in 1950.
- **Edge cases:** The deaf feel a rift in the chest, and rats flee it first. A silent seam of light is an Echo, never a door.
- **In play / story:** LM-17; Perfect Pitch reveals Resonance objects (CANON §5c); Lecterns (§2). **Chapters:** CH-P, CH-01, CH-12, CH-17.

### LAW-02 The Clefs
- **Plain:** Seven singing stones under the Left Bank anchor every rift. No traveller made them.
- **Mechanism:** Lenses of resonant *liais* shaped into plinths by the Frères around 1250 (CANON LAW-02; origin, pitches and panels in §4). Clef I (the Mouth) and Clef VII (the Heart) share one bed: what the Heart sends, the Mouth receives.
- **Examples:** The mark on Clef I, worn smooth by quarrymen's hands (CH-01); the plinth standing knee-deep at Clef II (CH-06).
- **Edge cases:** Nothing a quarryman or a painter owns can break a Clef; only the network's own collapse does, and only to the Heart (LAW-13). A Clef can be detuned (LAW-08). A fragment carried away becomes a far end. The "eighth stone" of tavern rumour is the Engine.
- **In play / story:** The Clef Map (KEY-19); detuning in CH-15. **Chapters:** CH-01, CH-08, CH-10, CH-12, CH-15, CH-16, CH-E2.

### LAW-03 Far ends and pitches
- **Plain:** In the future, a rift opens only where Clef stone lies, and always reaches the same distance back in time.
- **Mechanism:** A fragment's **offset** is set when it is cut, by its mass and grain, as a pendulum's period is set by its length; nobody chooses it. Each fragment sounds its parent Clef's pitch, shaded by its size (Delorme measures the shading in commas), and returns depend on that shading (LAW-05). Heart stone alone can be driven a semitone up, to the octave (§4).
- **Examples:** The table below, using the pitches from §4.
- **Edge cases:** A fragment opens wherever it lies; ground to powder, it falls silent (SQ-23). The Seoul Door's shard exists in the future only because the Heart shatters in 1833, which makes it part of knot (1), the Door (LAW-16). Brücke's 218-year far end will never exist in this history.
- **In play / story:** Explains why Delorme, Julien and Vane surfaced near Clef V, and Archon, Brücke and Min-jun near Clef I. **Chapters:** CH-08, CH-12, CH-14.

| Far end | Parent Clef / pitch | Offset | Crossings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clef V in situ (Paris Catacombs) | V / *la* (A) | 120 y | Delorme 1934→1814; Julien 1950→1830 |
| Clef II in situ (Val-de-Grâce vaults) | II / *mi* (E) | 96 y | Marthe 1918→1822 |
| London Stone (carried off in 1786) | V / *la*, one comma sharp | 161 y | Vane 1988→1827 |
| Seoul Door (the Heart's largest shard, set in the dial) | VII / *do♯* at rest; *ré* when driven (the signature) | 193 y | Archon 2012→1819; Min-jun 2026→1833 |
| Survey-tuned Door (Brücke's remembered history only) | VII, forced | 218 y | Brücke 2049→1831 |

### LAW-04 Opening and resonance
- **Plain:** Doors open only when Paris rings, by catastrophe or on purpose. A crossing leaves the traveller's door warm for a year and a day.
- **Mechanism:** A **Resonance Date** needs sustained mass sound across several quartiers for hours (Delorme's "Varga threshold"); a **strike** is a deliberate blow on the stone. Every far end mapped onto that instant opens (§5.2), unless the stroke is pitched to one far end alone (LAW-05).
- **Examples:** The register of rings (§5.2); Archon's 14:49 test, which opened the door it was struck to smother.
- **Edge cases:** The 5 June 1833 riot stays below the threshold. Min-jun's resonance would lapse on 6 Mar 1834, so **22 Dec 1833 is the last solstice at which he can be the living string** (§7). A crossing through a held window, like Lucile's, leaves nothing resonant behind. A *held* chord damps instead of opening (§5.2, 00:43).
- **In play / story:** Perfect Pitch's immunity to Unwritten ends in CH-17; BOSS-27. **Chapters:** CH-P, CH-01, CH-12, CH-16, CH-17.

### LAW-05 Arrival, the Passage Swoon, and return
- **Plain:** A struck rift knocks you out for hours and leaves you near your Clef, never on it. Going home means standing at the Heart while it is struck at your own pitch.
- **Mechanism:** Struck or collapsing rifts tear, which is the Swoon; a held window (LAW-13) does not (CANON LAW-05). The seam opens in the nearest open gallery to the far end's Clef. Struck at a far end's shaded pitch (for Heart stone, its driven *ré*), the Heart opens that far end alone.
- **Examples:** Min-jun swoons from 14:49 to about 18:45 (CH-01); Brücke swoons on the study floor after diving through the collapse (CANON LAW-24).
- **Edge cases:** The Grey Hand knew the Clef but not the gallery, so Mathurin (FIC-09) found him first. Every cabal member except Brücke could have gone home through Archon's Heart, and each refused (§8). Nothing passes on its own.
- **In play / story:** The opening of CH-01; the threshold under the Window Clock. **Chapters:** CH-01, CH-12, CH-17, CH-E1, CH-E2.

### LAW-06 Same date, same clock
- **Plain:** Both ends share the same date and world time; only the year differs. A day in Paris is a day missing in Seoul.
- **Mechanism:** Offsets are whole years and both ends run at the same rate, so clocks differ only by time zone (CANON LAW-06).
- **Examples:** 14:49 in Paris on 5 Mar 1833 is 23:40 KST on 5 Mar 2026; the Window's 07:51 sunrise is 16:41 KST (§11.1).
- **Edge cases:** No canon crossing or stay spans a 29 February. Delorme's margin asks "which calendar does the stone keep?", and no doc may answer it.
- **In play / story:** CH-17 cuts between the two clocks; Seo-yeon keeps his practice slot warm for all 291.7 days. **Chapters:** CH-P, CH-17, CH-E1, CH-E2 coda.

### LAW-07 Echoes (the monsters)
- **Plain:** Echoes are recordings of the city's recent grief and noise, printed onto stone by each ring. Defeating them hurts no one.
- **Mechanism:** A ring passes through stone the way Brücke's stylus passes through soot, and it leaves a groove. An Echo is that groove playing back, shaped by whatever made the noise. Echoes grow with each experiment and fade when the network dies.
- **Examples:** Ossuary (CH-01), Tocsin (CH-04), Tableau (CH-06), Lorelei (CH-11), residual (CH-E2).
- **Edge cases:** **An Echo may take the shape of an image, never of a body.** Le Chantre (BOSS-04) is a choirmaster's voice and office (cassock folds, a beating hand), never his face. Paganini's Shadow (BOSS-36) is the broadsheet devil-fiddler, and Paganini himself is alive. The Philosophers' Echo (BOSS-40) wears Houdon's statue of Voltaire and Rousseau's torch-bearing tomb. No Echo takes the shape of the plague dead (CANON §16e). The 2026 static automata are machines, not Echoes. **Lone singing rock.** The seven Clefs are France's only *network* and the only place a rift can open on this side. Other resonant outcrops exist, though: the Lorelei's slate, the chalk of the Étretat Needle, the sandstone of Fontainebleau. They have no far ends and open nothing. Each ring of the Paris network makes them sing in sympathy, printing local fear and legend (a drowning song, a wrecker's lantern) as Echoes that grow with Archon's experiments and fade with the network. The Lorelei sings a quarter-tone flat of *la*, outside the scale.
- **In play / story:** Area families (CANON §12); *Resonance* stuns them; the Fontainebleau Echo (GST-03), BOSS-37 and BOSS-39 are lone-rock Echoes. **Chapters:** CH-01 onward; CH-E2.

### LAW-08 Tablature
- **Plain:** Seven paintings by Mme Delorme tune the seven Clefs like pegs.
- **Mechanism:** Each panel hangs within resonance of its Clef. Pigment ground from Clef stone couples the canvas to the stone, and the hidden equation's proportions set the ratio, pulling each Clef onto one note of a scale on D (§4). *Ad Astra* (Panel VII) holds the Varga equation, which would drive the Heart up to its own **octave**. Without it, the seal needs a **living string**.
- **Examples:** *E = mc²* in Lucile's copy (CH-06, KEY-13); "a tuning system of seven" (CH-08); "H.V. 1934" (CH-11).
- **Edge cases:** **A copy carries the equation but not the stone,** so it tunes nothing. Delorme knew this and deposited her modello for the copyists, and a copy betrayed her. Her modelli, though, hold traces of Clef stone, which feed the Louvre's Tableau Echoes and BOSS-07's formula. A Clef can be detuned by destroying its panel, or by striking its plinth with one of Alkan's forks (KEY-32), which are ground to the tablature's *wolf* fifth. The party never burns a panel: they hang in three churches, an observatory, an ossuary and a school, all in public trust, and Delacroix will not destroy a painting. *Ad Astra* is **spent** opening the Heart gate; its pigment discharges and leaves a pale ground.
- **In play / story:** The Slip in BOSS-07; BOSS-25; the CH-15 plinths. **Chapters:** CH-06, CH-08, CH-10, CH-11, CH-15.

### LAW-09 The Engine (*Le Clavier des Âges*)
- **Plain:** A synthesizer made of slivers of the Clefs, and the only instrument that can hold the seal chord all night.
- **Mechanism:** Seven oscillator columns, each cut by night from one Clef, sounded by a three-manual console: a portable tablature (look: CANON LAW-09). Its first oscillator was tuned to Archon on 1 Jan 1833, so he ages at 1/10. Four horns record signatures.
- **Examples:** BOSS-28; its ruined keyboard holds the Window open (CH-17); the static automata (CH-E1).
- **Edge cases:** The last oscillator could in principle re-string a dead door. It fails in BOSS-30 because the shard in the dial answers only Min-jun. Ground to powder (SQ-23), it is silent for ever. It is the score's only synthesizer timbre (CANON §15a).
- **In play / story:** The parts of BOSS-28; SQ-23. **Chapters:** CH-12, CH-16, CH-17, CH-E1, CH-E2.

### LAW-10 Sealing from the 1833 side
- **Plain:** If the seal is held from the solstice instant to sunrise with Min-jun as its string, every door to the past dies, ring-bearers stop ageing, and he is bound alive in the Heart for ever.
- **Mechanism:** The Engine and the tablature supply seven notes; a living string with a resonant far end supplies the octave. Only Heart stone can be driven to the octave (§4), so the string must be a Heart-stone traveller: in December 1833 only Min-jun, the one possible substitute for Panel VII. A scale closed at its octave is a loop, and a loop leaks nothing to its far ends, so they die (§7).
- **Examples:** CH-16, from 00:43; Archon's Timetable (§13.22).
- **Edge cases:** *Why the solstice:* the network's yearly standing wave passes its node there, the only instant at which a chord can lock it. *Why sunrise:* the galleries are coldest and stillest at first light. A chord held through the night sets at that instant like mortar, or not at all. With no chord left to set, the same instant releases the dying network's stored ring, which is why the Window opens at 07:51 and not at the collapse. *Why 1833:* it is the last solstice of his resonance. *Parisians without a ring:* Stasis needs a coupling (a ring, the Engine, or the string).
- **In play / story:** The CH-12 Revelation; CH-16. **Chapters:** CH-12, CH-14, CH-16.

### LAW-11 Stasis and ageing ("immortality")
- **Plain:** Binding rings slow ageing to a quarter, but only inside the toll wall.
- **Mechanism:** A ring is a bead of Clef stone in black enamel. It couples the wearer to the network, damping their own far end and borrowing the network's slowness inside the Farmers-General wall (rates: CANON LAW-11).
- **Examples:** Archon has lived 57 years in a 47-year-old body (CANON §8a). Julien keeps his ring in his pocket.
- **Edge cases:** The cabal avoids the open road, which is why Grey Hand men and automata fight on the Act III routes. Taking a ring off restores normal ageing, but the far end stays damped. A native wearing one feels only cold metal: with no far end there is nothing to damp, so no Stasis.
- **In play / story:** The Act III enemy rosters; Archon's unlined face. **Chapters:** CH-10–CH-12, CH-16; the end cards.

### LAW-12 The signature
- **Plain:** A warm door's pitch rides inside its traveller's playing. Once it has been recorded, he is no longer needed alive.
- **Mechanism:** The signature is the resonant far end's driven pitch carried as an overtone, loudest when he plays with his whole self; Min-jun's sounds *ré* (§4). Brücke's horns scratch it into soot (KEY-23). A complete recording could replace the living string.
- **Examples:** CH-09: injured, he plays the left-hand prelude (REP-20) to survive, not to give, and Bercy records nothing usable. CH-13: the mid-concerto modulation spoils the cylinder (CANON LAW-12).
- **Edge cases:** Practice, hesitation and playing to order do not sound it. Brücke believes it sings loudest in a performer's own music, which makes the gala premiere the perfect trap. Recordings are always partial, so Archon must bind him in person.
- **In play / story:** BOSS-18; *Recorded Echo*; rungs 9 and 13. **Chapters:** CH-09, CH-13, CH-16.

### LAW-13 The death of the network and the Fleeting Window
- **Plain:** When the Engine falls, the network dies on one last note, his, and the Seoul Door opens for twelve minutes while someone plays.
- **Mechanism:** The half-struck chord folds into Archon and the Heart shatters. The dying network rings once at the pitch it was tuned to, Min-jun's driven *ré*, so only the Seoul Door answers; music played into the shards holds it open from 07:51 to 08:03 (CANON LAW-13).
- **Examples:** *La Musique des étoiles* on the ruined keyboard.
- **Edge cases:** No other traveller can ever go home. The Door is dead from 16:53 KST, apart from its decay hum (§11.1).
- **In play / story:** The Window Clock. **Chapters:** CH-16, CH-17.

### LAW-14 The Chronometric Survey and the Kang Disclosure
- **Plain:** Brücke remembers a future in which Min-jun came home alone, told everyone, and so created the agency that hunted travellers.
- **Mechanism:** An ossia history in which the Heart only cracked, so the Door stayed tunable (§9).
- **Examples:** The chain 2027 → 2031 → 2040 → 2048.
- **Edge cases:** 2049 material appears in at most four scenes, with no more than 6 boxes of exposition each; §13.11 and §13.27 sit inside two of those scenes (CH-07 and CH-E2).
- **In play / story:** BOSS-30's motive; KEY-38. **Chapters:** CH-07 (hint), CH-12, CH-E1 (duel), CH-E2 (dossier).

### LAW-15 The Score and the Ossia
- **Plain:** Heavily documented history resists change; small lives and attributions bend freely.
- **Mechanism:** Damping (§10).
- **Examples:** Berlioz still marries Harriet on 3 Oct 1833 after leaving his own wedding supper to lead a rescue. The Thalberg duel at the Austrian Embassy happens, but Rudolf Apponyi leaves the evening out of his journal: the ossia takes it, and the real duel of 31 Mar 1837 stays a bar-line (R-10).
- **Edge cases:** The 5 June 1833 riot is ossia, and history forgets it.
- **In play / story:** The end cards, and every Lantern Rule choice. **Chapters:** CH-04, CH-07, CH-09, CH-14, both epilogues.

### LAW-16 Knots (closed loops)
- **Plain:** Some things cause themselves. That is allowed, as long as nobody breaks the loop.
- **Mechanism:** A knot is a standing wave in history: self-consistent, with no first cause, damped from both ends. There are exactly four: the Door, the key's bow, the plaque, and the Arrival. The blank last page belongs to the Door's knot.
- **Examples:** Archon copied the sigil from the key in 2012; the friends shaped the bow after the sigil in 1834. Delacroix designed the Door from his CH-09 emblems and from Min-jun's description of a Door that Delacroix himself had designed, so the design has no first author.
- **Edge cases:** No branch breaks a knot, and the page arrives in 2026 blank. On the Seoul branch it is written only on 31 Dec 2026; on the Paris branch it is never written ("I saw it empty. If I fill it, I never came."). On the Paris branch, the old Min-jun helps crate the Door for his younger self. Composers' works are never knots.
- **In play / story:** The CH-P props; the jolt of recognition in CH-03; the REP-28 rules. **Chapters:** CH-P, CH-03, CH-09, CH-17, CH-E1, CH-E2.

### LAW-17 Secrecy in fiction
- **Plain:** The cabal already knows him. The meter measures how much of the future he spills into the public record.
- **Mechanism:** Witnesses, press items, police files, letters and rumours make up *the record*: what Paris remembers, and what an archivist in 2026 (or the Survey, in Brücke's history) could search. The cabal reads the record as risk and escalates. The Prefecture reads it as subversion. The Hour of Smoke turns Lucile's reports and the Watchers' sightings into rumour, which raises it further.
- **Examples:** *Le Corsaire* (+10, CH-03) prints "Ravel" for the first time, in the cabal's own ink (§13.05).
- **Edge cases:** The tiers name the city's attention, not the cabal's knowledge. An Unmasking is never a game over. The meter is retired in CH-E2 and inverted into the Spotlight Meter in CH-E1.
- **In play / story:** The candle; Onlookers; Bite Your Tongue. **Chapters:** CH-02–CH-14; CH-E1.

### LAW-18 The Lantern Rule
- **Plain:** He tells his friends who he is, never when they will die.
- **Mechanism:** A rule he chooses in CH-09. A date is a weight the hearer cannot set down, and a death is a bar-line no warning can move.
- **Examples:** To Chopin: "You will be played every day for two hundred years." He keeps silent about Alkan's withdrawal and *Le chemin de fer*. Farrenc hears that she was forgotten and then rediscovered, which is legacy, not death, and 1842 holds.
- **Edge cases:** He knows only famous deaths, so the ossia spares him Mère Gaudin's (FIC-02). In the Missions Étrangères corridor (§14.4) he knows that Bishop Bruguière will die in Tartary in 1835 without reaching Korea, and that French priests will be executed in Seoul in 1839, and he says nothing. End cards show no friend's death, except Min-jun's and Lucile's own on the Paris branch.
- **In play / story:** No menu ever offers a death date. **Chapters:** CH-09, CH-14, BQ-PC05, BQ-PC08, CH-E2.

### LAW-19 Foreknowledge and the living
- **Plain:** Foreknowledge may save a person; it may never make a profit.
- **Mechanism:** Single lives are ossia, so saving one moves no bar-line; profit is barred by the Vow.
- **Examples:** "Boil the water" (Bite Your Tongue). SQ-22: Rose Lambert leaves no. 12 rue Transnonain in March 1834, and the massacre still happens (shown in text and a still image only).
- **Edge cases:** He cannot stop April 1834. Penniless in CH-02, he could sell Herz a pianist's trick from 1905 for a month's rent, and doesn't; Archon's patents are the warning. He never learns how a life he saved goes on.
- **In play / story:** Bite Your Tongue; SQ-22. **Chapters:** CH-02 onward; CH-E2.

### LAW-20 Gift and theft: the reckoning and the Vow
- **Plain:** Bringing the future into the past is a gift when it is consented to, credited and unprofitable, and a theft otherwise.
- **Mechanism:** The Vow's clauses (consent, credit, no harm and no profit), sworn in CH-14.
- **Examples:** "*Gaspard de la nuit*, by Maurice Ravel."
- **Edge cases:** Lucile's Impressionism is Min-jun's own theft-shaped gift (§10).
- **In play / story:** Shadow 0–10; the "my own" option disappears after CH-14. **Chapters:** CH-03, CH-12, CH-14, BQ-PC08.

### LAW-21 Lucile's canvases in history
- **Plain:** Her 24 canvases are dangerous in 1833 and harmless to history afterwards.
- **Mechanism:** Filing (§10).
- **Examples:** "Circle of Corot" (Seoul branch); "the Aubray Problem" (Paris branch). *Le Charivari*'s fog joke (§13.09) survives only as one more jibe at a lady amateur, and Ingres's "weather" enters no record at all.
- **Edge cases:** Five burn in CH-11, so 19 survive for the Canvas Ledger.
- **In play / story:** Heat 2–4 per public showing. **Chapters:** CH-06, CH-10, CH-11, CH-14, both epilogues.

### LAW-22 The Hidden Study and the Pact of the Door
- **Plain:** His friends built the room beneath the annex, because he came.
- **Mechanism, inventory and history:** §6.
- **Examples:** The clue chain from CH-P to CH-17.
- **Edge cases:** Archon was inside it in 2012 (§13.30).
- **In play / story:** The CH-P puzzle; BOSS-30's arena; the CH-E2 coda. **Chapters:** CH-P, CH-05, CH-07, CH-09, CH-12, CH-14, CH-17, both epilogues.

### LAW-23 The Question
- **Plain:** "Will you come with me?" Her answer is computed from the whole game, never picked from a menu.
- **Mechanism:** Two weights decide it (rule: CANON LAW-23). *The gate is law, not mood.* Until Théo has a master and a guardian the law will accept, she cannot leave. The Code civil (art. 442) bars every woman but a mother or a female ascendant from a *tutelle* and from a family council, so Lucile is barred from her own brother's council; under art. 409 the *juge de paix* seats the late father's friends, and they name Aristide Farrenc *tuteur*. *Horizon is imagination:* how far her picture of her own life has already crossed the Window before he asks.
- **Examples:** The Sketchbook's last page is the in-world gauge, and the only one (secrecy_and_trust §5.7): Théo asleep while the gate is unmet; then rooftops with Théo, the Pont des Arts at dawn, an unfinished page, a ship on a horizon, towers of glass in snow.
- **Edge cases:** A tie gives NO, and so does an unmet gate ("I will not leave Théo with nothing."). The Early Truth is the largest single Wing because it gives her longest to imagine his century, and she never writes it down. On the NO path, if he stands 20 seconds at the threshold, she asks first: "Stay. Please." The Reverie of the Window plays an unmet SQ-07 as done, labelled "as it might have been": the score's ossia passage (§15), not a cheat.
- **In play / story:** Lessons, Window Talks, Sand's counsel and letter; the Reverie of the Window. **Chapters:** CH-06–CH-17.

## 4. The Seven Clefs

**Origin.** Around 1250 the quarrymen-monks of the Abbey of Sainte-Geneviève, the *Frères des Sept Pierres*, cut into seven lenses of *liais* that rang under the pick. They shaped each into a plinth and carved a hooked mark on it, which antiquarians later read as a mason's sign and Delorme read at once as a clef. They named only two stones, *Os* and *Cor*, the Mouth and the Heart, because those two came from one bed. At both they carved *OS ET COR UNIUS PETRAE · QUOD COR MITTIT OS RECIPIT · MCCL*, and later, in a rougher hand, *NOLI PERCUTERE IN NOCTE LONGA* (§13.24, §15). By 1300 the Frères are gone from the records. Their chronicle survives in what becomes the Bibliothèque Sainte-Geneviève.

**Pitch.** The raw pitches lie within a quarter-tone of the first seven notes of a scale on D. Delorme spent five years refusing to believe this was coincidence, then built her tablature outwards from the stone that brought her, Clef V. Tuned, the network sounds **ré, mi, fa♯, sol, la, si, do♯** (D E F♯ G A B C♯): a scale that stops on its leading tone. No Clef sings the upper D of itself. **The seal is the act of supplying that octave** (§7). It is also LM-01's eighth note, Lucile's note, the one that completes him (CANON §15c).

**Only Heart stone can resolve.** At rest the Heart, and every fragment of it, sounds *do♯*: that is what Morvan measured in 2012 (§13.01). Driven, either by *Ad Astra*'s Varga equation or by a living string whose far end is Heart stone, it is pulled a semitone up to the upper *ré*, the leading tone resolving. That is the octave the seal needs. It is why only a Heart-stone traveller can stand in for Panel VII, and why Min-jun's signature (his resonant far end, driven) sounds *ré* while the dial's shard at rest hums *do♯*. Archon's own far end is Heart stone too, but his ring has damped it. Through the Engine's first oscillator he can lean the Heart up to *ré* for a trial, as he did for ninety minutes before the 14:49 stroke on 5 Mar 1833; a damped string cannot hold a chord, though, so it can never close the seal (LAW-10). Between such trials the Heart sits at *do♯*.

### 6.3 How the Door works

The lock is a sympathetic escapement. Eight brass reeds behind the dial are tuned to the friends' eight-note theme, and each holds one tooth of a ratchet. Played in order, the notes free the teeth one by one, and the hands sweep from 7:51 to XII, where they line up into the key-shaped sigil and the keyhole opens. Théo knew that an untended piano sinks in pitch, so he made the reeds hear **the shape of the scale, not its height**, and an upright untuned for 130 years can still open the lock. Wrong notes simply free nothing. The lock is a spring latch: the key opens the leaf, but whenever the leaf swings shut the Door locks itself. Morvan always took the key out once the leaf was open and hung it back on its hook ("a guest does not leave his host's key in the door"), which is why in 2026 the Door is locked and the key is on the stand. Min-jun, a nervous student, leaves it in the dial, where CH-E1 finds it. The hub is Heart stone, which is why the Door answers only Min-jun's signature, its driven *ré* (§4, LAW-13), and why Brücke's oscillator cannot re-string it.

### 6.4 Who is "M."?

- **For the friends,** M. is Min-jun. The plaque is an order they gave themselves: build it, or he will never have come.
- **For Archon,** reading it by torchlight in 2012, M. was *Morvan*: a man told he would be a footnote had found a door that seemed to have waited 126 years for him. The player can connect the 2012 clipping (§13.01) with his CH-12 line (§12.2). No native ever learns the name Morvan (CANON §6, FIC-14).
- **On the Paris branch,** one of the hands crating the Door in 1886 is M.'s own: an old man packing a door for a young man who will use it to come, knowing he will never use it to leave.

### 7.2 The solstice protocol (night of Sat 21 – Sun 22 Dec 1833)

1. **19:00.** The Grey Hand take the crypt and the scaffold. "Gold is not to be admitted": Archon no longer trusts Delorme, which is why she is not in the chamber (LAW-13).
2. **22:00.** The Engine's seven columns are brought to pitch.

4. **00:43, the instant.** The **Stilled Hour** is struck: seven notes from the columns, coupled through whatever tablature still holds, plus the octave, Min-jun's driven *ré*, drawn from the living string. Inside the ward, time belongs to the chord. Archon's countdown runs in the ward's seconds while the night outside runs on, and each Voice of the Octave wins back a second (BOSS-27).
5. **00:43–07:51, the hold.** For seven hours and eight minutes the Engine must sustain the chord, retuning drifting Clefs from the bench. Archon fuses with it to do so (BOSS-28).
6. **07:51, the lock.** If the chord still stands at sunrise, the scale closes into a loop. Every far end dies; Stasis holds every coupled body inside the old wall; the string is bound into the Heart, conscious and unageing, for ever.

**Where it breaks.** The party has detuned Clefs I–VI (CH-15), so the chord can lock only into the chamber, not into the city. *Ad Astra* was spent at the gate, so no stone octave exists. The recording is partial, so Archon needs Min-jun in the room, and Min-jun comes with seven friends. The **Octave of the Lost Era**, eight living voices offering an octave freely, answers the octave Archon tried to take by binding, and it breaks the ward. The Engine collapses, the half-struck chord folds into the man fused to it, the Heart shatters, and the dying network rings once at the pitch it was tuned to: Min-jun's *ré*.

### 7.2a Why the quarries fuse two cities

From 22:00 the Engine drives the columns, and each tuned Clef prints onto the quarries what its stone has heard. Clefs I–II hold the oldest grooves, Lutetia's quarries and the barricades of 1830–32 (the Lutèce zones, Party 1). Clefs V–VI hold the ossuary's dead and Delorme's *Orpheus* (the Orpheus zones, Party 3). Clefs III–IV are coupled closest to the Heart: Geneviève shares its hill, and Urania is the anchor note the Engine tunes it against (§4). The Heart is being leaned toward Min-jun's *ré*, so these two print what it hears through its sympathy with his resonant far end: the Seoul he remembers (the Hanseong zones, Party 2). That is why only he can walk them, and why they are wrong in the way memory is wrong: Hanseong practice rooms opening off rue Bergère, a convenience store in the Palais-Royal arcade that stocks only what he misses. Because the Heart's print runs under all six, the seams bleed (a subway platform cut into the ossuary). Each plinth struck with a KEY-32 fork erases its zone; when all six are silent, only the Heart's *do♯* remains at the gate.

### 7.3 What success would have meant

No traveller could ever reach 1814–1833 again: no Survey, no Remediation, no correction of Archon's empire. The cabal would be ageless inside the wall and unable ever to leave it. Min-jun would be a living note in a stone chamber for ever. This is "trapping Min-jun forever" taken literally, and Archon's promised immortality bought with a city-sized cage (R-27).

The Pact's second rule left each of them lonely among the natives, so the cabal was the only room in Paris where they could speak plainly. That is why Julien stayed without wearing his ring, and why Vane's defection costs him his only friends.

**How it broke.** Archon broke the third rule ("he is not one of us until he chooses to stay"), and every later fracture follows from that. Vane's unsanctioned barricade (rung 5). Brücke's private lethal escalations (rungs 8 and 10). Julien's turn over the tensioner (CH-09). Marthe's, when she learned what the seal does to its string and saw a sick child used as bait (CH-11). Vane's, when he was ordered to kill Lucile (CH-14). By the solstice the Brotherhood is Archon, Brücke and the Grey Hand, with Delorme shut out of the chamber.

## 10. The Ethics of Changing History

### 10.1 The framework

The game never says that changing history is forbidden. It asks four questions of every act that carries the future into the past. **Bar-line or ossia?** (Is it heavily documented, or a small life or an attribution? LAW-15.) **Consent:** did the people affected choose to know? **Credit:** is the future's work named as its makers'? **Harm and profit:** who is hurt, and who gains wealth or power? History then responds in one of four ways. None is a conscious force; each is the weight of many independent records.

| Response | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Absorption** | A bar-line damps the change; events reroute to the same outcome | April 1834 happens with or without the party |
| **Fading** | A theft with no lineage decays, because nobody can build on what they cannot trace | Julien's chords die with the tapes |
| **Filing** | An anomaly is reclassified into a familiar category | Lucile's canvases become oil *esquisses* |
| **Never correction** | Nothing from the future polices the ossia | Marthe's patients live |

### 10.2 Worked examples

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Delorme's formulas** in drapery | Ossia | None | Stolen, except her own Varga equation, signed | A working instrument for the seal | **Filing**: an 1850s critic reads *E = mc²* as "a Byzantine monogram"; the canvases are judged "eccentric", and many burn in 1871 | Farrenc: "Functional, not vain. That is worse." |
| **Archon's patents** and counter-insurgency doctrine | Next to bar-lines | None | Through front men | Profit and power; the Vow's "no politics" broken | **Absorption and fading**: the tooling needs steels France will not make for decades; patents lapse, "strangely never adopted"; no documented outcome changes. End cards stay inside the Lantern Rule, so history's response is shown as legacy, never as punishment.

## 14. Paris, 1833: World Texture and Factions

### 14.1 Politics and police

Three years after the July Revolution, Louis-Philippe is "King of the French", and his *juste milieu* is squeezed from both sides. Marshal Soult heads the government; Thiers (Commerce and Public Works) is Delacroix's patron; Guizot's law on primary schools passes on 28 Jun 1833.
- **Republicans.** The Société des Droits de l'Homme has sections in every quarter; *Le National* (Armand Carrel) and *La Tribune* argue the cause and are prosecuted for it; students and workers mourn the Saint-Merry dead. Their grievances (the vote, wages, bread) are written fairly (CANON §16e).
- **Legitimists.** The Faubourg Saint-Germain keeps its shutters closed for the exiled Bourbons, embarrassed by the Duchesse de Berry's failed rising of 1832 and by her pregnancy at Blaye, explained in February 1833 as a secret marriage and ending in a daughter in May.
- **Saint-Simonians.** They are scattered since their 1832 trial, though some still wear the blue tunic buttoned at the back, so that each needs a brother to dress. In 1833 their "Companions of the Woman" leave for the East to seek a female Messiah.
- **Bonapartist nostalgia.** The Emperor's statue returns to the Vendôme column on 28 Jul 1833 (CH-06).
- **Order.** Prefect Gisquet (NPC-24) at the rue de Jérusalem, the *sergents de ville* (since 1829), the Municipal Guard, paid *mouchards*, press trials. Caricature is a weapon (Philipon's pear; Daumier, NPC-43, just out of Sainte-Pélagie). The National Guard is shopkeepers in uniforms bought at their own cost. The game never fights any of them (CANON §12).

### 14.2 The cholera's shadow

The epidemic of 1832 killed about 18,400 Parisians. In 1833 it is everywhere and nowhere: widows' black at the tavern, chairs nobody sits in, chloride of lime washed over doorsteps, ex-votos at Saint-Étienne-du-Mont, and the memory of April 1832, when crowds lynched men they took for poisoners. The rich fled; the poor could not. It is shown only through absence (CANON §16e), and it shapes the cast: Mère Gaudin's widowhood, Gilles Aubray's death, Théo's chest, Marthe's dispensary, and Archon's orphans' gala, grief turned into cover.

### 14.2b Poverty

Paris's poor are working people, and the game names them by trade. A seamstress earns about 1 F a day and a 2-lb loaf costs 7–8 sous, so bread alone takes close to half her wage. Water comes from a carrier at 1–2 sous a bucket, hauled up five flights. In December the winter coat goes to the Mont-de-Piété on the rue des Blancs-Manteaux, to be redeemed in spring if the work has held. The *chiffonniers* make their rounds by night with hook, basket and lantern; Nanette (FIC-25) is one, and she finds everything. On the Cité, lodging-houses let a place on the floor for a few sous a night. Children the cholera orphaned, or whose parents cannot feed them, go to the Hospice des Enfants-Trouvés on the rue d'Enfer. In the autumn of 1833 the journeymen (tailors, shoemakers, carpenters) strike for wages and hours, and the Prefecture answers with arrests (historical_notes_and_liberties to verify the dates). The rule is CANON §16e: named people with trades, never scenery and never a joke.

### 14.3 Daily life, money and travel

Wage, bread, rent, dinner, lesson, Pleyel, fiacre and diligence anchors are CANON §14's and are not repeated here. Two additions: a Paris daily costs about 80 F a year by subscription, so most people read it in cafés and *cabinets de lecture* (hence the Almanach's papers); the omnibus (since 1828) runs fixed lines for a few sous, and diligences leave from the Messageries royales, rue Notre-Dame-des-Victoires.

Oil *réverbères* swing on ropes over most streets; gas lights only the boulevards and the covered passages. Water comes from fountains or a carrier's buckets. Wine is cheaper beyond the toll wall, so on Sundays the faubourgs empty toward the *guinguettes* of Belleville and Montmartre. News travels by mail coach, and state messages by the Chappe semaphore on Saint-Pierre de Montmartre. Paris has no railway until 1837.

## 15. Secret and Post-Game Lore

- **Ossia and the Unwritten** (BOSS-31, Namsan, CH-E1; BOSS-34, the Dead Heart, CH-E2) are two faces of one thing: the history that did not happen, pushing through the last echo. Ossia's first bar shows the friends as they were in Brücke's history, where Lucile never met Min-jun. The middle phase of both is **the Shadow Virtuoso**, the Min-jun who called the future "my own" (fed by Shadow, LAW-20). Either fight yields §13.29.
- **The 1253 man** came in Saint Lucy's week (the old calendar's solstice), drew towers of glass and a door, and went into the Heart on the long night. The Frères' warning, *NOLI PERCUTERE IN NOCTE LONGA*, was carved afterwards: the first warning against striking the Heart at the solstice, written nearly six centuries before Archon read it as an instruction. There is no offset and no stone that remembers him, and **the game never answers who first heard the stones sing.**
- **Da Capo: The Unstruck Bell** (BOSS-43, NG+, LOC-P29) is the network's last unstruck note: the upper D no Clef sings of itself, the driven *ré* the seal tried to make of Min-jun (§4). It rings only for a player who comes round again from the beginning, which is why its phases replay the story's bosses: the score taken from the top.
- **For the attentive.**
  - The dossier names no watchmaker: in Brücke's history he was never in 1833, which is why that history had no Engine.
  - On the Seoul branch, a second Elias Brandt, aged 14, is growing up in Geneva, and Brücke never goes near him.
  - On the Paris branch the old Min-jun walks to Clef I every 5 March and taps the plinth twice, as he taps a fallboard. It never answers. In 1889 Lucile finds his pencilled "ré" on the stone and leaves it there.
  - Morvan's last page (§13.30) says "forty times there was nothing behind the leaf but brick". The Door opens onto brick whenever the network is silent: the friends built a door, not a hole, and only a ring turns it into a way through.
  - The Reverie of the Window is the score's ossia passage, "as it might have been", and is as true as the branch the player chose.
